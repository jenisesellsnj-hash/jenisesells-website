Ranch living on a park-like lot in Rambling Hills

This well-kept ranch sits on an oversized 0.39-acre lot in East Brunswick's Rambling Hills neighborhood. Inside you'll find four bedrooms, two full baths, hardwood floors, an eat-in kitchen, and a bright family room with a fireplace.
